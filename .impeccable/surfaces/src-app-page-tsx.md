## Direction contract
THESIS: Wayseeker is a dispensary for creative work. Every tool is a labelled drawer prescribed for one habit makers settle for, with its side effects written on the label. Refuses the tool-card directory and the headline-over-grid hero.
OWN-WORLD: Green lacquer cabinet (黛绿) with cool rice-paper label slips, brass pulls, one cinnabar seal red used only for strikes and stamps. Condensed Archivo caps for labels and headings, Noto Serif SC for Chinese display, tabular drawer numbers.
