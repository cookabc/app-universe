# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Designers, developers and creative workers who need to choose a suitable tool to finish a concrete task, rather than browse a product catalog sorted by popularity. Chinese and English users each get their own entry point.

## Product Purpose

Wayseeker helps you find the next tool that fits, starting from the task you are on. Success means you understand how the tools relate, you judge them from real previews and human curation, and you either visit a tool or share a reproducible exploration route.

## Positioning

Organised by workflow rather than by product category. You can explore along the route of finding direction, establishing a visual language, adding motion, studying real products and shipping; every tool keeps a human judgement of when to use it, what it replaces and what to watch for.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile web. The task map, real previews and notes update inside one exploration scene; keyboard, screen reader and reduced-motion support are required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep tool facts, bilingual content, real previews, detail links, search, reproducible random ordering and sharing. The brand is Wayseeker and the production domain is wayseeker.vercel.app; the previous page structure and visual language are not a constraint. 032 and 020 are entirely independent and do not take part in this product direction.

## Brand Commitments

Wayseeker's identity is the workflow map, the real tool scenes and human curation. It does not copy reference sites' palettes or layouts, and does not use a generic tool-card directory. The visuals may carry depth and spatial relationships, but content readability and native operation come first.

## Evidence on Hand

Bilingual facts and curation notes for 29 tools in `src/data/tools.ts`; real tool previews in `public/previews/`; reference roundups reviewed: Component Gallery, FeralUI, Libraries.dev, Impeccable, One Page Love, designmd.sh.

## Product Principles

Task before category. Scene before promotion. Real previews before decoration. Notes appear at the moment of decision. Exploration can return to the index, and can be reproduced and shared.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full keyboard and screen-reader operation, with the current tool and task explicit; non-current scenes leave the keyboard and screen-reader flow; reduced motion, long bilingual copy and mobile route switching are supported.
